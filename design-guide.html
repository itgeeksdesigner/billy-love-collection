Art direction

What the palette allows

The palette is low-saturation warm neutrals. That makes one rule non-negotiable: the garment is the only saturated thing in any frame.

Backgrounds that work

  • Warm plaster and limewash walls
  • Sand and oat seamless paper
  • Pale concrete, raw linen, light oak
  • Terracotta-cream, sunlit interiors
  • Anything between #FBF7F2 and #C4A88E

Backgrounds that fight it

  • Cool grey or pure white studio
  • Blue daylight, overcast city light
  • Foliage-heavy green scenes
  • Neon, nightclub, shop interiors
  • True black — your dark band is #2B2320, not black

Lighting: soft, diffused, warm — golden-hour window light or one large diffused key. Low contrast, gentle shadows, no hard flash.
Consistency: pick one skin-tone and white-balance treatment and apply it to every image. On a palette this quiet, a 200K drift between two adjacent cards reads as a mistake.
Accent discipline: the red on the camo tee and the pink on the tracksuit are the only strong colours on the page. Let the photography carry them — clay #C4A88E stays the UI accent.
